About NextWord AI

An advanced bilingual deep learning system for real-time text completion in English and Tamil.

🎯 Core Objectives

NextWord AI demonstrates how recurrent neural networks (RNNs) with Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) cells process sequence dynamics to infer subsequent words.

  • Real-time debounced sequence auto-completion
  • Dual-language support (English & Tamil)
  • Top-K Softmax probability rankings
  • Multi-word recursive sequence generation
